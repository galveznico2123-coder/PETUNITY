# PetUnity User Manual 🐾

Welcome to **PetUnity**! Your all-in-one community app dedicated to animal welfare, pet safety, and fun connections.

---

## 🚀 1. Getting Started

### **Sign Up & Login**
- **Create an Account:** Open the app and tap "Sign Up". Enter your email and create a password.
- **Verification:** Check your email for a verification link to activate your account.
- **Complete Profile:** Fill in your name and upload a profile picture to let the community know who you are.

---

## 🏠 2. Home Dashboard

The Home screen is your central hub for community activity.
- **Welcome Hero:** See your rank and progress towards the next membership level.
- **Live Stats:** Track how many pets have been helped, active reports, and pets near you.
- **Recent Reports:** Browse the latest community updates and alerts.
- **The "Plus" (+) Button:** Tap the floating plus button at the bottom right to quickly jump to the **Alert** section to report an incident.

---

## 🚨 3. Rescue Alerts (Emergency)

Use this feature to report an immediate animal emergency (e.g., an injured stray or a dangerous situation).

### **How to Send an Alert:**
1. Tap the **Alert** tab (or the **+** button on Home).
2. **Add a Photo:** Tap the camera icon. 
    - *Note: Our AI will check the photo. If it's not a pet or animal, you will see "No pet detected, please try again."*
3. **Location:** The app will automatically detect your location, or you can tap the location text to update it manually.
4. **Details:** Select the animal type, condition (Healthy/Injured), and provide a brief description.
5. **Urgency:** Check "Critical Emergency" if immediate help is needed.
6. **Send:** Tap "Send Rescue Alert" to notify nearby users.

---

## 🔍 4. Lost and Found

Help reunite pets with their families.
- **Lost Pets:** See reports of missing animals.
- **Found Pets:** See animals that have been found by others.
- **Add a Report:** Tap the plus button in the **Find** section.
    - Select if you are reporting a *Lost* or *Found* pet.
    - Provide the pet's name, breed, and contact info.
    - AI validation applies here too—only pet photos are allowed!

---

## 🐕 5. Play Date (Pet Matching)

Find companions for your pet!
1. **Create a Pet Persona:** Go to the **Play** tab and tap "Add Pet". Register your pet with their name, breed, age, and "vibe" (e.g., Energetic, Calm).
2. **Find Matches:** Swipe through potential playmates based on compatibility.
3. **Request a Playdate:** Tap on a pet profile you like, suggest a time and location, and send a request!

---

## 💬 6. Chat

Connect with other pet owners and rescuers.
- **Messaging:** Once a playdate is accepted or a report is discussed, use the Chat tab to coordinate.
- **Online Status:** See which community members are currently active.

---

## 🏆 7. Membership & Ranks

Be a hero and get rewarded!
- **Earn Points:** You earn points by helping the community (reporting found pets, responding to alerts).
- **Ranks:** Progress from "Pet Starter" 🐣 to "Legendary Protector" 🔥. Higher ranks show your commitment to animal safety.

---

## ⚙️ 8. Profile & Settings

- **Edit Profile:** Update your personal information and profile picture.
- **My Registered Pets:** Manage the pets you've added for playdates.
- **My Reports:** View and manage the alerts and lost/found reports you've created.

---

## ❓ 9. Troubleshooting & Tips

- **AI Validation Failed:** Ensure your pet is clearly visible in the photo. Avoid blurry images or photos with too many humans.
- **Location Issues:** Make sure GPS is enabled on your device and you have given PetUnity location permissions.
- **Notifications:** Enable notifications to receive urgent rescue alerts in your area.

---
