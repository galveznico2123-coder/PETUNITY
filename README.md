# PetUnity

> PetUnity is a community app dedicated to animal welfare. Report rescue emergencies, reunite lost pets, arrange playdates, and connect with fellow pet lovers — all in one place.

---

## What is PetUnity?

PetUnity is a mobile community platform built around animal welfare and pet safety. It brings together everyday people — pet owners, rescuers, and animal lovers — into a single app where they can respond to emergencies, report lost or found animals, and build meaningful connections with other pet owners nearby.

---

## Features

### Rescue Alerts
Report animal emergencies in real time. Add a photo, confirm your location, describe the situation, and notify nearby community members instantly. AI validation ensures only genuine animal photos are submitted.

### Lost & Found
Post and browse reports of missing or found pets. Include photos, breed details, and contact information to help reunite animals with their families as quickly as possible.

### Play Date (Pet Matching)
Create a profile for your pet and find compatible playmates nearby. Browse by breed, age, and personality vibe — then send a playdate request directly through the app.

### Chat
Coordinate with other community members once a playdate is confirmed or a rescue report is in progress. See who's online and respond in real time.

### Membership & Ranks
Earn points by contributing to the community — responding to alerts, reporting found pets, and more. Progress through ranks from **Pet Starter** to **Legendary Protector**.

## Getting Started

1. Download PetUnity and tap **Sign Up**
2. Verify your email address
3. Complete your profile with a name and photo
4. Explore the home dashboard to see live community activity

---

## AI Photo Validation

All photo submissions — for rescue alerts and lost/found reports — are checked by an AI model to confirm an animal is present. If no pet is detected, you will be prompted to try again with a clearer image.

## Troubleshooting

**AI validation keeps failing**
Make sure your pet is clearly visible and centered in the frame. Avoid blurry images or shots with too many people in frame.

**Location not detecting**
Go to your device settings and confirm that GPS is enabled and that PetUnity has been granted location permissions.

**Not receiving alerts**
Check that notifications are enabled for PetUnity in your device's notification settings.
